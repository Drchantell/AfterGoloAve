## Description — current iOS preview only

**Meet me on the Avenue.**

AfterGlowAve is a playful city-inspired experience for adults. Discover a directory of destinations, keep favorites handy, and enjoy quick game-night classics.

**Explore cities**
Browse destinations around the world and search by city, state or country.

**Save your favorites**
Tap the heart to remember places you want to explore again. Saved locations stay on your device.

**Play together**
Challenge a friend to a two-player Tic-Tac-Toe match on one device, or roll a pair of virtual dice.

**Made for adults**
An introductory age-confirmation screen asks visitors to attest that they are at least 18.

This preview does not include live matchmaking, video chat, cloud accounts, online multiplayer, subscriptions, or paid content. Those features require further development and review before they can be advertised as available.

## Reviewer walkthrough — CURRENT PREVIEW
1. Launch the app and confirm age 18+.
2. Tap **Explore**, search for Charlotte, and switch the country filter.
3. Tap a heart to save a destination; open **Favorites** to view or remove it.
4. Open **Arcade**, play local Tic-Tac-Toe, reset, and roll the dice.
5. Open **About** to review the current limitations and reset age confirmation.

No login is required for the native iOS preview. Do **not** provide web-app credentials as though they unlock iOS features.
